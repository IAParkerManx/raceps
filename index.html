Before you start

Mount the phone rigidly with a clear view of the sky. Stop on flat road and stay still until Ready. Do several runs, in both directions where safe and legal. Full-throttle distance runs belong on a closed track or drag strip.

Install on your phone

You're using the home screen app.

Adding RacePS to your home screen opens it full screen, like an app.

iPhone, Safari
  1. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open RacePS from its new icon.
iPhone, Chrome
  1. Tap the Share button next to the address bar.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open RacePS from its new icon.
Android, Chrome
  1. Tap the menu, the three dots at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Confirm, then open RacePS from its new icon.

On iPhone, the home screen app keeps its own saved runs, separate from the browser. To move runs across, tap Back up in History, then restore the file in the home screen app.

Name on your summary

Optional. Stays on this phone and isn't sent in diagnostics.

Changing the car starts a new session. Runs you've already saved keep their name.

Speed units

Changes the speedometer, peak and trap speeds. Timing names like 0-100 km/h stay the same, and voice callouts stay in km/h.

Timing mode

From a set speed, the clock starts the moment you pass it, like 60-100 or 100-200 on a Dragy. You can start from a stop or from rolling below that speed. Both ends of each time are read from GPS, so its delay cancels out.

Voice

Callouts use a recorded voice that works in the home screen app. The voice chosen here is only used if those recordings can't play.

Saved runs

A backup holds your run times, speed traces, and driver and car names. It has no GPS position. Your browser can clear saved runs if the app goes unused, so keep a copy somewhere safe.
